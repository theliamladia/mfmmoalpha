    📖 Wiki

    A quick rundown of every page and how to use it.

    🛣️ Da Skreetz

    Instant-payout hustles on a 10 second cooldown. Work is safe and pays $2–$10. Slut pays $5–$60 with a 30% chance of getting robbed for the same amount, and nudges your Alliance toward Bad. Crime pays $100–$1,000 with a 30% chance of landing in Jail; getting caught sends your Alliance straight to Bad (or worse) instantly, and your criminal record grows, adding a year to every future sentence (from any crime) until you pay it down with Community Service.

    🏬 Milos Market

    Plan-it Fitness burns weight for Speed/Defense gains — pick a Steroid cycle (Mild, Standard, or Heavy) for bigger gains at the cost of worse Roid Jail odds and longer sentences. Pete's Pies and Luke's Maxxerstore trade weight and cash for Looks; pricier items always cost more per point, never less, so there's no "cheap trap" option. Cosmetixxx sells titles and gacha crates (OPEN BETA $5,000, GOOD® Season 1 $10,000, Anima Crate $4,500, Counterfinish Crate $3,000). Max every stat to 100 to earn the PEAK CIVILIAN title. Looks speeds up job skill training, Height and Speed help you in a fight, Speed/Defense help you dodge a bust on the job, and high HP means you keep more cash when you lose a fight.

    🎰 Midas Casino

    Convert Floydbucks to Chips at the Cashier (Buy Max/Cash Out Max convert your whole balance in one click), then play Blackjack, Slots, or Roulette. Blackjack supports Double Down and Split on matching pairs, plus 1/2 Bet and Max Bet quick-set buttons. Slots has three machines with different min bets and payouts — Lone Slotvivor (3 reels), Zeus: King of Storms (4 reels, bigger payouts), and ELITE Slots (6 reels, up to a 10,000x jackpot). Roulette is single-player: place any mix of straight, red/black, even/odd, high/low, dozen, and column bets, and the wheel spins automatically 10 seconds after your first bet. Chips only exist in the Casino — cash back out at the Cashier when you're done.

    🌆 New Milos City

    Hustles splits into Good Hustles (small steady legal cash, Neutral alliance or better) and Bad Hustles (drug running, dealing, and robbery — Bad alliance required, high risk of jail). Both job tracks use a promotion ladder: as your average skill climbs, each rank has its own pay band (the floor rises faster than the ceiling, so higher ranks pay more and more consistently) plus a shorter cooldown. Reaching Supervisor/Lieutenant unlocks a job-specific perk — Milos11 knocks 20% off Pete's Pies, Pete'sza Delivery grants a permanent +2 Speed, Krogue Wrestler Gear unlocks the exclusive Wrestling Gear Store, Getaway Driver cuts your bust chance, and The Fence knocks 15% off the Gun Club. Combat is turn-based: Punch (reliable), Heavy Strike (bigger damage, can whiff), Guard (blocks most damage, can riposte), and Weapon Attack (biggest damage if you're carrying a weapon) — Height and equipped weapons/gear boost your Attack, Speed lets you dodge outright, HP protects your cash if you lose, and a win has a chance to grant a small permanent Attack or HP boost. Crime is a set of fixed-payout crimes (Shoplifting up through Grand Theft Auto) where Attack and Speed lower your odds of getting caught; getting caught here (or anywhere) snaps your Alliance straight to Bad and escalates every future sentence until you spend cash on Community Service. City Hall handles renames, marriage, and the Gun Safety Course. NMC Gun Club sells guns, ammo, melee weapons (no license needed), a shooting range, and carry licenses — you need the Gun Safety License from City Hall before you can buy a gun. Bank keeps your cash safe from robbery, lets you upgrade to hold more, and offers a credit card billed every 24 real-life hours. Morals Center lets you pick a standing stance — Accept Ricardo, Renounce Ricardo, or Invoke Neutrality — that quietly nudges your Alliance every 10 seconds, even on other pages. Milos Trading Network is the player marketplace: pick an item to list from a picker organized by category — and for titles, by crate and rarity — and once it sells the Floydbucks land in your account, the sale logs to your MTN history, and you get a notification. New Milos Penitentiary shows who's currently locked up (and for what), plus a recent-arrests history — post bail or send commissary money for their books. Coinflip lets you create a cash-wager lobby and call heads or tails; another player joins and takes the other side, then the winner takes both wagers. Milos Outlook Farms grows weed/coke plots for sale, with a real risk of confiscation or an intercepted shipment. MyCrypto Setup mines Floydcoin (FC) passively based on your rig tier, tradeable for cash at a floating price; a robbery has a small chance to drain some of your hot-wallet FC, so move some into the Cold Storage Module (upgradeable capacity) to keep it safe. Altcoins lets you mint your own rug-pull coin — whoever holds the plurality can dump the whole pool for a big payout, or offer a fair pro-rata buyout once it fully sells out. Investors Center is the stock market — see its own Wiki entry below.

    📊 Investors Center

    A stock market inside New Milos City with 10 tickers across five volatility tiers, from the ultra-stable UNITS the Company down to meme stocks like PORK Judaism and Luqunior Ent. Prices tick every 32 seconds and are identical for every player on the server at any instant — trading itself never moves the price. Pick a stock from the dropdown to see its sidebar (price, your holding, avg cost) and a price chart with 1H/1D/1W/1M/All timeframes; hover the chart for the exact price and time at any point. Buying and selling both carry a small stock tax (shown live as you type a quantity), with Buy Max/Sell Max for one-click full-size trades. Your Portfolio tracks every open position's live profit/loss, and the Performance Log shows your total realized + unrealized profit plus a full paginated trade history. InvestorsChat at the bottom is a separate chat room from New Milos City's main chat — real players post alongside randomly generated trader bots, and some of the bots' "news" about a ticker is real (and actually moves that stock's price) while the rest is just rumor. There's no way to tell which is which except by watching what actually happens to the price afterward.

    🎒 Character

    Inventory is split into Licenses, Items, and Cosmetics — Cosmetics groups your owned titles into per-crate sections (Anima Crate, Counterfinish Crate, etc.) that expand into Regular/Prestige sub-tabs, Regular sorted by rarity and Prestige by prestige level. Equipment has an 8-slot board — guns and melee weapons in the holster/open-carry/melee slots, and exclusive Wrestling Gear (from the Krogue Wrestler Gear job) in the helmet/chest/pants/feet slots for extra combat stats. Carrying a gun without the right license or permit risks arrest — check the law banner at the top of New Milos City. Melee weapons (brass knuckles, knives) need no license and carry no arrest risk. Skills shows XP bars for your current jobs, weapon skills, and drug-dealer reputation. Alignment shows exactly where you sit on the Good–Bad spectrum plus your overall level/stat progress toward PEAK CIVILIAN.

    ⛓️ Jail

    Only reachable if you're arrested — all other pages grey out. Serve Time removes 1 year every 10 seconds. Hire Lawyer pays cash for immediate release. Doing time doesn't have to be dead time: Yard Workout is a free, repeatable Attack/Defense gain, Yard Fight is an instant-resolve scrap against another inmate for a stat boost (or a cash shakedown if you lose), and Contraband lets you smuggle in melee weapons and drugs at a markup so they're waiting in your Inventory when you're out. Repeat offenses raise your criminal record and lengthen future sentences — use Community Service (New Milos City → Crime) once you're free to pay it down. New Milos City → New Milos Penitentiary is the public side of all this: see who's currently locked up (and for what), post their bail, or send them money for their books.

    🏆 The Collectibility Update: Three Graders, Cert Numbers, the Pop Report & the Set Registry

    2026-08-20
    • 🏥 NEW — two rival graders open up shop. New Milos Grading is no longer the only game in town. Milos Market → Grading District now hosts three counters sharing the same 4 slots: CCG (Cheap Cool Grading) at $2,000–$8,000, NMG unchanged at $5,000–$20,000, and MGA (Milos Grading Association) at $15,000–$60,000. Each one has its own slab case, its own cert series, and its own reputation.
    • 💎 MGA rolls SUBGAINS. Every MGA slab is itemised into four component scores — Gloss, Stitching, Aura, Drip — each within 2 of the main grade. A main-grade 10 with all four subgains at 10 is a BLACK LABEL: roughly 1 in 11 MGA 10s, with its own black-and-gold case. It is the rarest thing the grading system can produce.
    • 💰 CCG is cheap, and the market knows it. CCG slabs cost 60% less to make and are valued at half everywhere value is shown — Est. value captions and the KOLLECTOR leaderboard. They're affordable slabs to flex, not investments. MGA slabs are valued exactly the same as NMG: what a Black Label is worth is between you and whoever wants it.
    • 🔢 NEW — every slab now has a cert number. NMG #000482, MGA #000017, CCG #000009. Printed on the slab, permanent, and independent per grader. Every slab you already own has been retroactively certified — you keep your place in line, and the oldest collections got the lowest numbers.
    • 🏷️ NEW — First Edition. A slab graded while its crate is still active is stamped FIRST EDITION. Existing slabs were stamped on the same rule, generously: if the crate is still open today, your slab is a First Edition. Once a crate archives, nothing graded after that gets the stamp — ever.
    • 📊 NEW — the Population Report. Grading District → Pop Report: every title, every grade, every living slab in the city, split by grader and grouped by crate. A POP 1 is the only one that exists anywhere. Cracking a slab removes it from the census permanently — which now means something.
    • 🔍 NEW — Cert Lookup. Type any cert number to see the slab, who holds it right now, its subgains, and its full provenance — every grade, regrade, and sale it has ever been through, with prices.
    • ♻️ Regrading is now part of a slab's story. A slab goes back to the grader that graded it (an MGA slab regrades at MGA prices with fresh subgains). The cert keeps its number and the regrade is written into its history — so a 6 that became a 10 shows exactly that. MGA regrades run $39,000/$48,000/$66,000; CCG's are a flat 2× the grading fee at $4,000/$8,000/$16,000.
    • 🔨 Cracking is permanent, and public. Same $50,000, same result — but the cert is now retired in the registry forever and the slab leaves the population count. The confirm dialog tells you which cert number you're about to destroy.
    • 🏦 Legacy slabs can still get subgains. Nothing graded before today has subgains recorded. Regrading an old slab at MGA is how you give it a set — and the only route a long-time collector has to a Black Label on a title they've held for months.
    • 📚 NEW — the Set Registry. Slab every title in a crate — any grader counts, foils and prestiges count, your best copy per title is what's scored — and your set registers on a public leaderboard ranked by GPA. Permanent achievement titles: REGISTRY COLLECTOR for your first complete set, MASTER SET at GPA 8+, and PERFECT SET for an all-10 set, which nobody has and possibly nobody ever will. The registry is empty at launch. First name on the board keeps it.
    • 🦉 One for the historians: when every existing slab was certified, the numbering ran oldest-first — and cert NMG #000001 landed on a Presidential Auto graded 10. The rarest pull in the game holds cert number one.
