🌈 Enjoying & Variety

Variety and Enjoying are two new subtraits coming to New Milos City.

Enjoying

Pins your victim to the ground and dominates them — success is based on your Looks and Attack. The victim gets the same timeout screen as Sliming, but for just 1 minute, and it's announced for everyone in New Milos Chat:

"I'm {enjoyed_user} and I just got enjoyed by {enjoyer_user}, I couldn't do a THING!"

Victim: +20% Variety. The one doing the enjoying: +5% Variety.

Chill in Chat

Typing "chill" in chat automatically bumps your Variety by +1%.

Lowering Variety

You can renounce Variety at the Morals Center to bring your percentage back down.

⚠️ Dangers of Variety

Enjoying is a great humiliation tool — but racking up Variety yourself comes with real consequences. Your Alliance changes as it climbs.

20%
Allegiance: Various
  • 20% debuff on all money-related jobs (except Slut)
  • Immediately fired from your job until Variety comes back down

  • 50% debuff on all money-related jobs (except Slut)
  • Treated permanently as Dirty Bad until Variety is lowered

  • 90% debuff on all money-related jobs (except Slut)
  • Unable to buy cosmetics
100%

  • 100% debuff on all money-related jobs (except Slut)
  • 1-day Variety timeout, with a chat announcement
